# Terms of Use for Linoleum

Effective date: 2026-08-23.

Linoleum is an optional Neo Angband tile-rendering mod and loose tile-pack format, with no separate hosted service. It can add alternate graphics rows and render supplied or player-created loose tile packs. It is disabled until enabled, and disabling it leaves the base game's graphics unchanged.
